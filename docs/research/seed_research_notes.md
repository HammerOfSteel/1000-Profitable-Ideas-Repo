## 2) Sub-category candidate: Compliance Workflows

### Working thesis
Compliance-heavy small teams repeatedly spend time and money on evidence gathering, policy tracking, audit preparation, and vendor/risk documentation. This creates recurring workflow pain that is narrow enough for focused product wedges.

### Real evidence collected

#### Strong evidence
- Sprinto pricing page directly frames the problem space in recurring operational modules:
  - automated evidence collection
  - audit planning and management
  - employee policy acknowledgement campaigns
  - onboarding/offboarding workflows
  - vendor discovery and reviews
  - periodic risk assessments
  - compliance health and gap reports

This strongly supports the claim that compliance work is not a one-screen use case; it is a bundle of recurring operational jobs.

#### Moderate evidence
- G2 search result coverage for audit management software in SMB contexts
- Search result coverage for compliance software comparisons, pricing, and review ecosystems

### What this currently supports
- **Demand:** moderate to strong
- **Willingness to pay:** strong
- **Competition gap:** not yet strong enough; still need direct review-complaint or simpler-tool wedge evidence
- **Distribution:** not yet strong enough; need real communities or buyer-access paths
- **Feasibility:** conceptually strong for narrower workflow slices, but needs more specific wedge definition
