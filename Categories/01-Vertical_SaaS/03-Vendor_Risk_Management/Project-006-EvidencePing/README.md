## 📖 Overview
Helps lean compliance teams send structured reminders for expiring questionnaires, policies, certificates, and supporting files so vendor reviews do not get stuck in manual follow-up loops.

## 🛑 The Problem
Vendor reviews often stall because required evidence is missing, outdated, or scattered across inboxes, forcing repeated manual chasing and delaying approvals or reassessments.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Primary buyer:** Compliance leads chasing third-party evidence
- **Primary operator/user:** Security and GRC generalists collecting vendor documentation
- **Secondary users/stakeholders:** Expand during blueprint refinement
- **Target market:** SMBs and mid-market teams handling vendor onboarding, questionnaires, and third-party oversight
