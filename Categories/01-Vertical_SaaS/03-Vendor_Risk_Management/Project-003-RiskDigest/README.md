## 📖 Overview
Packages questionnaire findings, monitoring signals, and remediation status into concise recurring reports for operators, executives, and audit stakeholders.

## 🛑 The Problem
Even when teams collect vendor-risk data, they still spend manual effort turning findings into digestible status updates, exception logs, and executive-ready summaries.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Primary buyer:** Compliance leads preparing stakeholder updates
- **Primary operator/user:** Security and GRC generalists reporting third-party risk posture
- **Secondary users/stakeholders:** Expand during blueprint refinement
- **Target market:** SMBs and mid-market teams handling vendor onboarding, questionnaires, and third-party oversight
