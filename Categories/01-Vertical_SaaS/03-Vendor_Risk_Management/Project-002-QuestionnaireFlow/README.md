## 📖 Overview
Helps small compliance and security teams collect vendor responses, reuse prior answers, and move from questionnaire intake to decision faster.

## 🛑 The Problem
Vendor questionnaire reviews are repetitive, manual, and spread across inboxes, spreadsheets, and shared drives, slowing down approvals and creating inconsistent risk reviews.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Primary buyer:** Compliance managers reviewing third-party questionnaires
- **Primary operator/user:** Security analysts at SMBs and mid-market firms
- **Secondary users/stakeholders:** Expand during blueprint refinement
- **Target market:** SMBs and mid-market teams handling vendor onboarding, questionnaires, and third-party oversight
