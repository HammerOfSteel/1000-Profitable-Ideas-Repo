## 📖 Overview
Turns questionnaire gaps, monitoring findings, and exception decisions into a lightweight remediation workflow for lean teams that need clear ownership and status visibility.

## 🛑 The Problem
Teams may complete vendor reviews, but they still struggle to assign fixes, track exceptions, and report remediation progress across spreadsheets, emails, and ad hoc follow-ups.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Primary buyer:** Compliance leads managing vendor follow-up
- **Primary operator/user:** Security and GRC generalists handling exceptions
- **Secondary users/stakeholders:** Expand during blueprint refinement
- **Target market:** SMBs and mid-market teams handling vendor onboarding, questionnaires, and third-party oversight
- **Where they already gather:**
- LinkedIn outbound
- GRC and security communities
- Compliance consultant referrals
- **Willingness-to-pay signal:** Direct competitor positioning treats remediation, exception handling, and ongoing oversight as core workflow value, which supports paid demand for a lighter execution layer.
