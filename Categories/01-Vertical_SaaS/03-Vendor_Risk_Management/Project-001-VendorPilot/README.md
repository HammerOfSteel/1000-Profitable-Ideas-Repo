## 📖 Overview
Centralizes vendor intake, questionnaires, risk review, and ongoing monitoring without the overhead of an enterprise TPRM platform.

## 🛑 The Problem
Lean teams often manage vendor reviews across spreadsheets, email threads, and ad hoc evidence requests, creating slow onboarding and weak ongoing oversight.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Primary buyer:** Operations managers handling vendor approvals
