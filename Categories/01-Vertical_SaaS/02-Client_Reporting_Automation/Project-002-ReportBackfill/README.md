## 📖 Overview
Helps agencies reconstruct weekly or monthly client-ready reporting packages from messy internal inputs without rebuilding everything by hand.

## 🛑 The Problem
Client updates often depend on scattered screenshots, channel notes, metrics exports, and task updates, forcing account teams to manually assemble the same report story every cycle.

- **Primary buyer:** Agency account managers
- **Primary operator/user:** Freelance marketers and operators
- **Secondary users/stakeholders:** Expand during blueprint refinement
