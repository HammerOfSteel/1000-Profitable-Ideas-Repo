## 📖 Overview
Captures meeting notes without intrusive meeting bots and emphasizes local or privacy-preserving workflows for sensitive discussions.

## 🛑 The Problem
Teams in confidential environments often want AI meeting assistance but reject bot-based recording and cloud-heavy data handling because of privacy, trust, or compliance concerns.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Primary buyer:** Consultants handling sensitive client calls
- **Primary operator/user:** Legal and healthcare-adjacent operators
- **Secondary users/stakeholders:** Expand during blueprint refinement
- **Target market:** Consultants, agencies, founders, operators, and internal teams with frequent virtual meetings
- **Where they already gather:**
- Privacy-focused productivity communities
- LinkedIn content around secure AI workflows
- Consultant and operator newsletters
- **Willingness-to-pay signal:** Privacy-sensitive teams already evaluate bot-free and privacy-first note takers, suggesting a willingness to pay for better trust, control, and compliance posture.
