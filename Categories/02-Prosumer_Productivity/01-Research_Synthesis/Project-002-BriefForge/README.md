## 📖 Overview
Packages scattered notes, links, and observations into structured briefs for consultants, founders, and strategy operators who need polished outputs fast.

## 🛑 The Problem
Knowledge workers often gather strong research but still spend manual effort reshaping raw notes into usable briefs, memos, and recommendation documents.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Primary buyer:** Independent consultants delivering research-backed recommendations
- **Primary operator/user:** Solo founders evaluating markets
- **Secondary users/stakeholders:** Expand during blueprint refinement
- **Target market:** Researchers, strategists, consultants, and AI-assisted creators

- LinkedIn content

- YouTube workflow demos
- **Willingness-to-pay signal:** Users already pay for note-taking and knowledge-work tools, and the final synthesis step is directly tied to client work, decision-making, and time savings.
